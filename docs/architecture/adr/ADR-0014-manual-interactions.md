## Context
Phase 3 slice 8 (owner approval 2026-10-29) implements FR-083, FR-084 and FR-085 only. The Call and WhatsApp launchers (FR-080/081), call intents and the post-call prompt (FR-082), provider calls and events (Phase 6), recordings, the script version on an interaction (FR-098), editing interactions, the list's last-interaction fields and offline drafts are **not** built. **Legal gate G5 stays open**: nothing here clears the product for real customer data or outbound contact.

Sources: FR-083 ("channel, direction, outcome, approximate duration (optional), notes, next action… `source = MANUAL`… within 1 tap of the lead page"), FR-084 ("requirement updates and next follow-up… one submit, one transaction"), FR-085 ("Manual vs provider-confirmed entries are visually distinct… 'Logged by user' vs 'Verified by provider'"), workflow B (idempotent submit, logging without a call intent), lead-lifecycle §2 and §3, domain model §Interaction, role matrix §4.2, ADR-0012, and the owner's decisions of 2026-10-29 (Q1–Q5 and defaults).

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Source / why | Enforcement / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Record:** `interaction` holds one contact a person reports: channel (`PHONE_NATIVE`, `WHATSAPP_APP_CALL`, `WHATSAPP_APP_MESSAGE`, `SMS`, `EMAIL`, `IN_PERSON`), direction, outcome and its group, when it happened, an optional estimated duration (minutes, calls and meetings only, never mixed with provider duration), notes, `contact_status_at_log`, an idempotency key and a request fingerprint. `source = MANUAL`, `status = LOGGED` only. **`contact_status_at_log` is the lead's contact status when the interaction is logged, immediately before any status change the same submission makes** (decision 10). It is not the status at the reported time of the contact and is not proof of it (decision 7, known gaps). | FR-083; domain model (codes reused so Phase 6 widens rather than renames); review M2 | CHECKs in 0017; `interaction_guard` requires it to equal the lead's status at insert, which precedes the status event |
| 2 | **Outcomes:** the lifecycle §3 codes and groups; `UNKNOWN_NOT_CONNECTED` is provider-only and not offered. Outcomes must fit the channel and direction (for example "No answer" only for outbound calls, "Message sent" only for outbound messages, "Meeting held" only in person, number outcomes only on phone-based channels). | lead-lifecycle §3 | `rules.ts` and the `interaction_outcome_fits` CHECK (same rule) |
| 3 | **Append-only:** SELECT and INSERT only (no editing; `interaction.edit` is deferred). Actor = session user and `logged_at` = transaction time, stamped by RLS. | Owner approval; audit conventions | Grants, RLS; tests |
| 4 | **Timeline (FR-085):** the insert trigger writes an `INTERACTION` timeline entry in the same transaction (channel, direction, outcome, group, source, reported time, duration). Notes stay on the interaction and are shown in the timeline only to people with `interaction.read`. Entries are ordered by logged time (existing order); the reported time is shown separately. Badge "Logged by user" for `MANUAL`; "Verified by provider" is reserved for `PROVIDER` rows, which cannot exist yet. | FR-085; owner default (ordering) | Trigger; component and integration tests |
| 5 | **One transaction (FR-084):** lock the lead (`FOR UPDATE`), validate everything, then write requirement changes, the interaction, the automatic stage change, the outcome-driven contact status change and the follow-up change. Any refusal rolls all of it back. To make that possible, the existing commands were split into a transaction-taking core used by both the old command and the sheet: `applyRequirements`, `applyStageChange`, `recordContactStatusChange`, `scheduleFollowUpIn`, `rescheduleFollowUpIn`, `completeFollowUpIn`. None opens its own transaction. | Owner approval ("all commit or all roll back"; "refactor them to accept the caller's transaction") | Rollback test (a failing follow-up step leaves no interaction, stage, requirements or timeline rows) |
| 6 | **Idempotency (amended after review M1):** the form carries a UUID made when it is shown; it is kept after a refusal and replaced after a save. Unique `(org_id, user_id, client_idempotency_key)`. Each interaction stores `request_fingerprint`: SHA-256 of a canonical, normalised form of **every input that can change a side effect** (key, lead, version, channel, direction, outcome, reported time, duration, notes, invalid-number confirmation, contact status confirmation, reason, method and evidence, the requirement patch, and the follow-up task, version, done flag, next time, title and reschedule reason). Only the digest is stored. Same key, same lead and same fingerprint: the first result, nothing written again. Same key and lead, different fingerprint: conflict, nothing written. Same key on another lead: conflict. A row from before 0018 (no fingerprint): conflict, because the full original submission cannot be verified. The lead scope is checked first, so someone who lost access gets "not found", never an earlier result. Concurrent submissions with one key wait on the lead lock. | Workflow B; NFR-094; review M1 | `fingerprint.ts`; 0018 (format CHECK; required on new rows by trigger); tests: identical retry, changed outcome, requirements and follow-up, concurrent identical, legacy row, lost scope |
| 7 | **Do-Not-Contact, outbound (Q2; review M2 option a):** refused in the app (`outboundEligibility` for the field message, then `assertOutboundAllowed` on the locked row) and in the database (`interaction_guard`), using the lead's status **when the interaction is logged**, not a status reconstructed at the reported `occurred_at`. No override, no historical exception and no way to record an outbound contact on a lead that is Do-Not-Contact now. | ADR-0012; owner decisions Q2 and M2 (a) | Tests for every channel, app and direct insert |
| 8 | **Do-Not-Contact, inbound (Q1):** contact the customer started may be logged. The status does not change and no permission to contact follows; outbound stays refused. | Owner decision Q1 | Tests |
| 9 | **Invalid number:** outbound contact needs an explicit confirmation, stored on the record (`invalid_number_confirmed`); inbound needs none. Integration channels are untouched (still blocked by ADR-0012). New manual outbound channel codes `MANUAL_MESSAGE` (SMS, email) and `MANUAL_IN_PERSON` follow the same rule as `MANUAL_CALL`. | ADR-0012 decision 9 | App, DB guard; tests |
| 10 | **Outcome-driven status changes (Q3):** "Spoke: asked not to be contacted" → Do-Not-Contact; "Wrong number" / "Invalid number" → Invalid. Only with an explicit confirmation and the contact status workflow's details (reason; for Do-Not-Contact also how they asked; evidence optional), written as the existing `lead_contact_status_event` (origin `MANUAL`) in the same transaction. A change that would repeat or weaken the status (Do-Not-Contact → Invalid) is not made and the sheet says so; the interaction is still recorded. Nothing lifts Do-Not-Contact; nothing is treated as consent. | Owner decision Q3; ADR-0012 | Tests |

| 12 | **Requirements (Q4; amended after review L1):** the existing requirement fields in a collapsible section, prefilled. An ordinary log with untouched requirements sends none, so another person's newer requirement change is neither overwritten nor a reason to refuse the log. A real edit on a lead whose version changed since the form was shown is refused as a conflict (nothing saved); the form keeps every entry and offers "Undo my requirement changes". No new lead fields. | Owner decisions Q4 and L1 | Tests: untouched requirements with a concurrent change succeed and keep the newer data; a stale edit is refused |
| 13 | **Follow-up:** the caller's own open follow-up can be marked done and/or a next one set (done + next = complete and schedule; next alone = reschedule, with the FR-102 reason rule); without one, an optional new follow-up. Won and Lost leads take none. | Owner default; FR-100/102/108 | Tests |
| 14 | **When it happened:** defaults to now; not in the future; at most seven days ago (org time zone in the form; CHECK against `logged_at` in the database). | Owner default | Unit, integration, CHECK |
| 15 | **Permissions:** `interaction.create` and `interaction.read` O / O / T / S (support: read with an approved grant; HR: none), plus `lead.update` in scope for requirement or status changes and `task.*` for follow-ups. | 16 | **Script version shown (FR-098; added 2026-10-29, migration 0019):** an interaction stores `script_version_id`, the exact published `call_script_version` that was on screen **the last time the lead-page script drawer was open** on that page visit before Save (owner decision). ## Known gaps and open items
- **Logging-time contact status (review M2, option a). These are known audit limitations, not evidence of legal compliance; G5 stays open:**
  - Outbound logging is evaluated with the status when the interaction is logged. A contact made while the lead was OK cannot be recorded once the lead has become Do-Not-Contact, even though it happened before.
  - A contact made while the lead was Do-Not-Contact can be logged after an authorised lift, and its row then shows `contact_status_at_log = OK`. Only the contact status history (event times) can show that the reported time fell inside a Do-Not-Contact period.
  - Direction is self-reported: an outbound contact could be recorded as one the customer started. Manual records cannot prevent this.
  - `contact_status_at_log` must not be presented as the lead's status at the time of the contact.
- **Fingerprint (review M1):** the digest is derived from personal data (notes, reasons), with the random idempotency key as part of its input. It is not reversible in practice but is still derived data, and falls under the same retention question as the row. Requirement and follow-up details are verified only through the digest; they are not stored on the interaction.
- **Review L5:** a reschedule reason without a reschedule, or a follow-up title without a new follow-up time, is refused with a field message instead of being ignored. Field messages are collected, never overwritten.
- **Audit gap (Q2):** an outbound contact made to a Do-Not-Contact lead cannot be recorded at all. Breaches that already happened therefore leave no record in the CRM. A separate, flagged breach-reporting workflow needs its own proposal and approval.
- **G5 / retention (not decided here):** how long interactions and their notes are kept (the domain model's "3 years online" is unconfirmed), how erasure (FR-061/FR-171) treats interaction notes, whether a "customer said stop" outcome is sufficient withdrawal evidence (C4) and for how long it is kept, and DPDP Rule 8(3) versus NFR-053 audit retention. No retention period, purge or erasure path exists for `interaction`. Its RESTRICT foreign key means an erasure feature must clear or anonymise interactions before deleting a lead.
- **Interactions are timeline records, not audit-log entries** (workflow B). Stage and contact status changes they cause are audited as usual.
- **Not built:** launchers and call intents (FR-080/081/082), editing (`interaction.edit`), `last_interaction_at` / `last_outcome` on the lead and list, offline drafts (NFR-094 beyond idempotent retries), provider rows.
- **NFR-003** (save ≤ 500 ms p95): a local production-build indication only (docs/PHASE-3-NFR-003-BENCHMARK.md); formal acceptance needs the staging k6 run and OD-02.
- **FR-098 limits:** the version is not yet shown on the timeline or in a coaching report (no report exists). A version is recorded only from the drawer on the same page visit: a script read on another page (`/app/scripts`) or in an earlier visit is not recorded.
