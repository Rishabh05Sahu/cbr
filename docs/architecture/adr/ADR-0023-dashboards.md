## Known limitations and open points
- **Performance (NFR-005, FR-132):** queries run live (no `kpi_daily_*` aggregates, per metrics §5 "introduce only when p95 > 2 s"). **Not measured at the base workload** (250k leads, 90-day range). The only timing is a local dev-server page load of the organisation dashboard on small synthetic crm_dev data: about 1.4–1.7 s per load, including Turbopack dev rendering. That is not a measurement at the base workload, so the under-2-second target is **unverified**.
- **Lead scope vs §2 attribution (decided 2026-10-30, decision 5):** figures follow the viewer's current access scope, so leads reassigned away drop out of a person's own cohorts and activity counts. Accepted by the owner: personal figures can understate a person's history, and that is stated on the dashboard and drill-downs. Historical credit stays in the activity and audit records and in the organisation view.
- **Merge chains:** records are mapped to the survivor one level deep. A lead merged into a lead that was itself merged later drops out until re-mapped.
- **Short spells (§2)** are judged by the spell's duration and the user's interactions on that lead. "Ended by reassignment" is read as "ended".
- **K18 attribution:** a closed task is credited to its assignee at closing (the model has no task-assignment history, as metrics K18 notes).
- **Not in scope:** CSV export (FR-135, Should; needs `report.export` and auditing), scheduled emails (FR-134, Phase 7), K14–K17 and K21–K24 (later phases), "working days available" normalisation (needs leave).
