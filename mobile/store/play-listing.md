## Main store listing
- **App name:** CBR CRM
- **Short description (80 chars max):** Leads, calls and follow-ups for the Crystal Bricks & Roofs sales team.
- **Full description:**

> CBR CRM is the mobile app of CBR Property CRM, the customer-relationship system of Crystal Bricks & Roofs, Dubai. It is for invited staff only: you need an account from your organisation to sign in.
>
> • My Day – overdue, today's and upcoming follow-ups in one list
> • Leads – search your leads, filter by due date, intent and stage
> • One-tap Call and WhatsApp, then log the outcome as soon as you come back to the app
> • Call scripts while you talk, and the lead's requirements at a glance
> • Phone numbers stay masked when your organisation requires it; every reveal is recorded
> • Secure: two-step verification for owners and admins, and screenshots are blocked
>
> The app does not read your call log, contacts, SMS or location.

## Data safety

- **Is all data encrypted in transit?** Yes.
- **Can users request deletion?** Yes: https://crm.crystalbricksroofs.com/account-deletion
